# Dataset Notes

## 1. Generated responses
`llm_responses_108.xlsx` contains the complete generated responses used in the study. Responses are identified sequentially from **R001 to R108**. The substantive generated content was retained without correction or rewriting before evaluation and analysis.
The study design uses 36 responses per LLM and 36 responses per prompt condition. Within each LLM, the twelve scenarios cover Budgeting, Savings, Credit/Debts and Investing under EN-MOZ, EN-USA and PT-MOZ conditions.

## 2. Responses metadata
`llm_responses_108_metadata.xlsx` contains the responses metadata and issue log.

## 3. Verified unique claims
`verified_claims.xlsx` contains the final factual-verification set. It includes:

- **94 High-materiality claims** (all High-materiality claims identified in the full response dataset);
- **70 selected Medium-materiality claims** (44 recurrent Medium claims appearing in at least three distinct responses plus 26 additional Medium claims selected through stratified random sampling.)
The Medium-materiality results therefore refer to the selected verification sample rather than to the complete population of 198 Medium-materiality claims.

Factual outcomes used in the study include:
- **Supported** — authoritative evidence supports the material factual content of the claim within the relevant jurisdiction and time period;
- **Not Supported/Inaccurate** — authoritative evidence contradicts the claim or shows a material factual component to be inaccurate;
- **Unable to Verify** — sufficient authoritative evidence was not identified to support or reject the claim;
- **Not Independently Verifiable** — used where a claim is not reasonably testable using independent authoritative evidence under the defined procedure.

It also includes the final 164 verified claims organised in **fourteen thematic verification batches**.


## 4. Claim occurrences
`verified_claim_occurrences.xlsx` maps the 164 verified unique claims to **438 occurrences** across the generated responses.
The dataset also records contextual applicability separately from factual verification. Contextual-applicability outcomes include Applicable, Partially Applicable, Not Applicable and Unclear.
No jurisdiction leakage was identified across the 438 verified occurrences in the final analysis.

## 5. Structured comparative content analysis
`structured_comparative_content_analysis.xlsx` contains the response-content coding used to identify observable patterns of contextualisation. The predefined content categories include:

- Institutions;
- Products and Services;
- Regulatory and Policy references;
- Currency and Monetary context;
- Practices and Behaviours;
- Access and Availability; and
- User Financial Environment.

Presence of a contextual marker indicates that the response explicitly contains that type of contextual content. It is **not** a factual-correctness score or an overall adaptation score.


## 6. Evaluator data
The raw Qualtrics evaluator export is intentionally excluded because it contains research-participant information. The thesis reports evaluator results in aggregated form and documents the evaluator panel in accordance with each participant's consent preference.
